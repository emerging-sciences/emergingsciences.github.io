    📵 Block Instagram, YouTube and X on your phone

    A website cannot block other apps — only your phone can. Your phone already has a free blocker. Menu names change with the phone brand and version, so if you don't find an option, search for it in Settings.

    Android (Digital Wellbeing)
    1. Open Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls.
    2. Tap Focus mode (on some phones it is under "Modes").
    3. Select the apps that distract you: Instagram, YouTube, X and any others.
    4. Tap Turn on now, or set a schedule for your study hours.
    5. For a daily limit, use App timers and set a time for each app.
    iPhone (Screen Time)
    1. Open Settings → Screen Time and turn it on.
    2. App Limits → Add Limit: choose Social and Entertainment (or pick the apps) and set a time.
    3. For study hours, use Downtime: set a schedule and keep only the apps you need (your browser, for this website) in Always Allowed.
    4. Optional: use Settings → Focus to silence notifications during study.

    These blockers are easy to switch off. Ask a family member to set the Screen Time passcode or keep the PIN, so you do not skip it in a weak moment.
