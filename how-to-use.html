❓ Focus Mode kaise use karein

Ye page ek-ek cheez simple bhasha me samjhata hai. 5 minute me poora samajh aa jayega.

Pehle ek idea: "Round"

Ek round matlab bina ruke padhna, aur uske baad chhota break. Jaise 25 minute padhai + 5 minute aaram. Isse dimaag thakta nahi, aur padhai beech me chhoot-ti nahi. Is tareeke ko Pomodoro kehte hain.

Jaldi shuru karne ke 5 step

1
Site ke upar header me timer wala icon ⏱ dabao. Focus Mode page khulega.
2
25 / 5 chuno (pehli baar ke liye yahi sabse achha hai).
3
Apna phone Do Not Disturb par karo. Instagram, YouTube, X band rakho (neeche bataya hai kaise).
4
▶ Start dabao. Ab tum Lectures ya Material kholke padh sakte ho. Ek chhota timer screen par dikhta rahega aur chalta rahega.
5
Awaaz aaye to round poora. 5 minute break lo, phir dobara Start.

Page ki har cheez ka kaam

25 / 5, 45 / 10, 60 / 10
Padhai ke minute / break ke minute. 25 / 5 = 25 min padhai, 5 min break. Ye button tabhi chalte hain jab timer band ho.
Focus aur Break box
Apne hisaab se minute likh sakte ho (padhai 1 se 180, break 1 se 60).
Subject
Maths, Physics jaisa subject chuno. Isse timer par asar nahi padta. Ye sirf neeche "Recent rounds" me likha jata hai, taaki yaad rahe kya padha.
Bada gol clock
Beech me bacha hua time. Neeche likha aata hai Ready (shuru nahi hua), Focus (padhai chal rahi hai), Paused (ruka hua), Break (aaram). Ring padhai me neeli aur break me hari hoti hai.
▶ Start / ⏸ Pause
Start se timer shuru. Pause se ruk jata hai aur bacha hua time yaad rehta hai. Phir button Resume ban jata hai.
↺ Reset
Timer wapas poore time par aa jata hai. Agar 1 minute se zyada padha tha, to wo round "stopped early" ke naam se list me save ho jata hai.
⏭ Skip break
Sirf break me dikhta hai. Break chhod ke agle round ke liye taiyar karta hai. Khud shuru nahi hota, tumhe Start dabana hota hai.
⛶ Zen mode
Header aur neeche ka menu chhupa deta hai aur full screen karta hai, taaki dhyan na bhatke. Bahar aane ke liye ✕ Exit zen dabao.

Round poora hone par kya hota hai

  1. Awaaz aati hai, phone vibrate karta hai, aur "Round complete 🎉" likha aata hai.
  2. Break apne aap shuru ho jata hai.
  3. Break khatam hone par phir awaaz aati hai. Agla round tum khud Start dabake shuru karte ho.

3 tick-box (options)

Keep my screen on
Timer chalte waqt phone ki screen band nahi hoti. Ye har browser me nahi chalta. Jahan na chale wahan kuch nahi hota.
Notify me
Round khatam hone par notification aata hai. Tick karne par browser permission maangega, Allow dabana.
Strict mode
Agar tum 30 second se zyada website se bahar rahe (doosra app, doosra website, ya screen lock), to round wahin khatam. Jitna padha utna ginta hai, par wo round "done" nahi mana jata.

"Bahar jaane" ki ginti (Leave detection)

Timer chalte waqt agar tumne website chhodkar doosra app ya doosra website khola, to site ko pata chal jata hai. Site ke andar Lectures ya Material kholna bahar jaana nahi hai.

  • Jitni der website se bahar rahe, utna time padhai me nahi ginta.
  • Wapas aane par likha aata hai: "You were away for 40 seconds. Back to study 💪"
  • 3 second se zyada bahar rahe tabhi "times you left" badhta hai. Ek-do second ki galti nahi ginte.

Ye rokta nahi, sirf sach dikhata hai. Isse apni aadat samajh aati hai.

"Today" card: aaj ka hisaab

Bar + "12 / 30 min"
Roz ke goal me se kitna ho gaya. Poora hone par "goal reached 🎉".
minutes studied
Aaj ka total padhai ka time. Isme lecture dekhne ka time aur focus round ka time, dono judte hain.
🔥 day streak
Lagataar kitne din tumne goal poora kiya. Ek din chhoota to streak shuru se.
rounds done
Aaj ke poore hue rounds.
times you left
Aaj kitni baar page se bahar gaye.

Roz ka goal pehle 30 minute hota hai. Isko site ki Settings me badal sakte ho.

Last 7 days aur Recent rounds

Last 7 days: pichle 7 din ka har din ka padhai ka time (minute me). Hari bar matlab us din goal poora hua. Recent rounds: pichle 6 rounds: tareekh, minute, subject aur kitni baar bahar gaye.

📵 Instagram, YouTube, X block karna

Website doosre apps band nahi kar sakti. Ye kaam sirf phone kar sakta hai. Phone me free setting hoti hai:

Android (Digital Wellbeing)
  1. Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls kholo.
  2. Focus mode dabao (kuch phones me "Modes" me hota hai).
  3. Wo apps chuno jo bhatkate hain: Instagram, YouTube, X.
  4. Turn on now dabao, ya padhai ke time ka schedule lagao.
  5. Roz ki limit ke liye App timers me har app ka time set karo.
iPhone (Screen Time)
  1. Settings → Screen Time kholo aur on karo.
  2. App Limits → Add Limit: Social aur Entertainment (ya apps) chuno aur time lagao.
  3. Padhai ke time ke liye Downtime lagao. Always Allowed me sirf wo apps rakho jo zaroori hain (is website ke liye apna browser).
  4. Chaho to Settings → Focus se notifications bhi chup karao.

Menu ke naam phone ke brand aur version ke hisaab se alag ho sakte hain. Na mile to Settings me "Focus" ya "Screen Time" search karo. Ye setting aasani se band ho jati hai, isliye ghar ke kisi bade se passcode lagwa lo.

Ek example din

Rahul ne 25 / 5 chuna, subject Mathematics rakha aur Start dabaya. 10 minute baad usne WhatsApp khola aur 1 minute baad wapas aaya. Page ne likha "away for 60 seconds" aur times you left 1 ho gaya. Wo ek minute padhai me nahi ginta. Round poora hone par awaaz aayi, break shuru hua, aur roz ke 30 minute ke goal ki bar aage badh gayi.

Zaroori baatein (FAQ)

Lecture ya Material kholne par timer ruk jata hai?

Nahi. Timer apne aap chalta rehta hai. Site ke kisi bhi page par neeche ek chhota timer dikhta hai. Usse ⏸ se rok sakte ho, ✕ se band kar sakte ho, ya uspar dabake Focus page par ja sakte ho. Lecture ya PDF padhne ka time bhi ginta hai.

Browser ya site band kar diya to?

Jitni der site band rahi, utna time padhai me nahi ginta, aur wo "bahar gaye" me judta hai. Agar tab tak round ka time poora ho chuka tha, to round khatam maana jata hai.

Meri history kahan save hoti hai?

Sirf tumhare phone ke browser me. Koi server ya account nahi hai. Doosre phone ya laptop par alag dikhega, aur browser ka data saaf karne par history mit jayegi.

Screen lock hone par kya hota hai?

Site background me jati hai, to wo "bahar gaya" ginta hai aur wo time padhai me nahi judta. Isliye Keep my screen on tick rakho.

Ye Instagram wagairah ko khud band karta hai?

Nahi. Upar ka 📵 hissa padho. Wo phone ki apni setting se hota hai.

Ye sab free hai?

Haan. Koi paise, login ya subscription nahi.
