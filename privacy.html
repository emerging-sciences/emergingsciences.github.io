Short and simple. Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Who we are

Emerging Sciences is a free study website and Android app made by Piyush Agarwal for MSc entrance exam students (IIT JAM, CUET PG, TIFR, GATE, CSIR NET and others). Questions about privacy: message us on Telegram.

What we do not ask for

  • No account, no login, no password.
  • No name, phone number, email or address is needed to use the website or the app.
  • No advertising. At the moment we do not use analytics either.

What is saved on your own device

Your lectures watched, saved items, study time, daily goal, streak, Focus Mode rounds and settings are saved only in your own browser or app storage. We do not receive them. They are not shared between your phone and laptop. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, they are deleted. You can also delete them from the Settings on the home page.

Outside services this website uses

To work, the website loads content from other services. When you open a page, your browser connects to them, and they can see things like your IP address, as with any website. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • YouTube plays the lecture videos. YouTube may set its own cookies.
  • Google Drive shows and downloads the PDF notes and papers.
  • Google Sheets holds the list of lectures and material, which the website reads.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts.
  • Botpress runs the doubt chatbot. Anything you type in the chat is sent to that service, so please do not share personal details such as phone numbers, passwords or bank details in it.

Supporting us with money

Payments are made through your own UPI app. We only see what any receiver sees: the amount, the time, and the name and UPI ID of the payer. We never ask for your PIN or OTP. On the Support page we publish a public account book of money received and spent. For received money we show only the first name that you write in the payment note, or "Anonymous". We do not publish phone numbers, UPI IDs or full names unless you ask us to.

The Android app

The app shows the same content as the website. It asks only for Internet permission. It never needs your OTP, bank details or login. Install it only from the button on this website.

Children

The website is for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
